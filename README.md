> **"Fast. Clean. Optimized. Smart tech shouldn't be locked down."**

Hey there! 👋 I am **revWhiteShadow** (widely recognized across developer and low-level modding communities as **revWS**), an independent systems engineer, Linux kernel researcher, low-level Android enthusiast, and cybersecurity analyst based in **Bangladesh 🇧🇩**.

The **"rev"** prefix in my name represents **Revolutionary** — because I don't just accept defaults or follow trends. I build tools that break artificial barriers, eliminate throttling, and give users full ownership of their hardware.

My passion lives at the intersection of bare-metal optimization and clean engineering: analyzing OS kernel behavior, rewriting CPU/GPU scheduling heuristics for silky-smooth responsiveness, crafting custom Android Magisk/KernelSU modules, hardening apps through ethical pentesting, and designing resilient full-stack systems with **zero background telemetry**.

And when I step away from the compiler and debuggers? I'm a hardcore gamer. Whether it's mastering frame data in arcade fighting games, parrying bosses in brutal Soulslikes, or optimizing game engines down to the millisecond on budget hardware — if a system can be pushed further, I'll find a way to do it.

#### 🎯 What Drives Me:
- 🚀 **Pushing Silicon to its True Potential**: OEM thermal throttling and unoptimized defaults hold hardware back. Through direct `sysfs`/`procfs` kernel tuning, custom governor heuristics, and I/O calibration, I build setups that run with zero jitter and deterministic speed.
- 🛡️ **Absolute Privacy & Zero Telemetry**: Your device belongs to you. I reject invasive ad trackers, analytics SDKs, and bloatware. Clean, transparent, telemetry-free software is a non-negotiable principle.
- 🕹️ **Gamer & Low-Level Modder at Heart**: Gaming and systems engineering feed into each other. From frame-perfect fighting game combos to custom trainers, memory inspection, and emulator optimization — gaming is part of my engineering DNA.
- 📖 **Knowledge Sharing & Open Research**: I've authored over 100+ technical guides and benchmarks covering kernel tuning, emulation architecture, and low-level internals so other developers can optimize their own machines.
